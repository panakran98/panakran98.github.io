About

I am a PhD student in the Department of Electrical and Computer Engineering at the University of Virginia, starting in Fall 2026, where I am fortunate to be advised by Professor Nikos Sidiropoulos.

Before UVA, I earned my Diploma in Electrical & Computer Engineering from the National Technical University of Athens (NTUA), Greece, specializing in Information Technology, with a diploma thesis on diffusion models for image analysis in medical imaging and autonomous driving, supervised by Professor Stefanos Kollias. I also worked as a software engineer building backend and machine learning systems for telecom and defense applications.
